# Part 4 – Deliverables, projects and activities

This part records the **programmatic** side of the Roadmap: what your country has committed to deliver and which activities are implementing it.

## 21. Open the Deliverables app

1. In the side menu, under **Apps**, click **Deliverables**.
2. One card per pillar appears, showing the number of **Deliverables** and **Stories** already recorded for your country.
3. Click **Manage** on the pillar you want to work on. (The green **Manage Stories** button is explained in Part 5.)

The pillar page shows:

- **left column – Deliverables:** the list of your deliverables with an **Add** button;
- **right column – Projects:** the projects of the deliverable you selected (empty until you click **Manage** on a deliverable).

## 22. Add or edit a deliverable

**Add**

1. Click **Add** in the **Deliverables** box (or **Add Deliverable** if the list is empty).
2. In the **Create Deliverable** window enter:
   - **Name** (required) – for example **National Youth Employment Strategy adopted**.
   - **Description** (optional).
3. Click **Create Deliverable**.
4. The deliverable appears in the left column with **Projects: 0**.

**Edit**

1. Click the **pencil** icon next to the deliverable.
2. Change the name or description and click **Update**.

## 23. Add or edit a project / activity

1. In the left column click **Manage** (cog icon) on the deliverable. It is highlighted and the right column now shows **Projects** for it.
2. Click **Add Project**.
3. In the **Add Project** window fill in:
   - **Name** (required) – name of the project, programme or activity.
   - **Start Date** and **End Date** – the implementation period.
   - **Planned Budget ($)** and **Executed Budget ($)** – amounts in US dollars.
   - **Results table** – one row per expected result:
     - **Planned Results** – what was planned,
     - **Achieved Results** – what was actually achieved,
     - **Achievement Status** – **Not Achieved**, **Partially Achieved** or **Fully Achieved**.
     - Click **Add Result** for more rows, or the **−** button to remove a row.
4. Click **Add Project**.
5. The project card appears with badges (**Has Budget**, **Planned Period**), the **Implementation Period**, the planned **Budget** and the results table.

**Edit a project:** click **Edit** on its card, change the fields and click **Update**.

> **Note** – A result row is only saved when **both** **Planned Results** and **Achieved Results** are filled in.

## 24. How programmatic scores work

Each **project** receives a score out of 4:

| Component | Points |
|---|---|
| An implementation period (start and end dates) is recorded | 1 |
| A planned budget is recorded | 1 |
| Average of the result statuses (Not Achieved = 0, Partially Achieved = 1, Fully Achieved = 2) | 0 to 2 |

- **Deliverable score** = average of its project scores.
- **Pillar deliverable score** (the badge on the Pillars Dashboard) = average of the deliverable scores.
- Colours (default **Pillar Settings**, adjustable by the administrator): **green** for 4 and above, **yellow** for 2 to 3.9, **red** below 2.

> **Tip** – To improve a project's score, record its period, its budget and honest result statuses.
