# Part 8 – Other pages

## 30. Releases (what's new)

If **Releases** appears in your menu, it lists the platform versions with their release notes grouped as **New Features**, **Improvements**, **Bug Fixes**, **Security** and **Breaking Changes**. Use **Search release notes…** to find a topic. Check it after each platform update.

## 31. The public website

Anyone can open the platform address without logging in and see:

- the **home page** with the four pillars and partner logos, with an **Explore Dashboard** button that leads to the login page;
- **Success Stories** – all published stories, with search, a country filter and sorting;
- **About** – background on the platform and its partners (UNECA and UNFPA);
- the language switch (EN / FR) and the **Sign In** link.
