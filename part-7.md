# Part 7 – Managing users in your country

Country administrators can create accounts for colleagues in their own country.

## 29. View, add, edit and remove users

**Menu:** **Set up → Users & Roles → Users**.

**View**

- The list shows only users of your country: **Name**, **Email Address**, **Role** and **Country**.
- Use **Search users…** to filter by name.

**Add a user**

1. Click **Add User**.
2. In **Create New User** enter:
   - **Name**
   - **Email Address** (must be unique; the person logs in with it)
   - **Password** and **Confirm Password** (at least 8 characters)
   - **Country** – locked to your country
   - **Role** – choose the role for this person
3. Click **Create**.
4. Communicate the email and password to the new user securely and ask them to change the password from their **Profile** at first login.

**Edit a user**

1. Click the **pencil** icon on the user's row.
2. Change the name, email or password (leave the password fields empty to keep the current one).
3. Click **Update**.

**Remove a user**

1. Click the **trash** icon on the user's row.
2. The account is deactivated immediately. You cannot delete your own account.

> **Important** – Only create accounts for people who need to enter or view your country's data, and remove accounts of people who leave.
