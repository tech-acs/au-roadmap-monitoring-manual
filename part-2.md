# Part 2 – Your dashboards

All dashboards are **read-only**. They display what has been entered through the Indicators and Deliverables apps (Parts 3 and 4). If a dashboard shows **No data**, the data has not been entered yet for that year.

## 7. Per Country dashboard (your home page)

**Menu:** **Per Country**. This page opens automatically after login.

### 7.1 What you see

1. **Year selector** (top). Choose the year you want to look at. By default the previous year is selected. The year you pick here is remembered for 30 days and is also used on other screens.

2. **Country selector** (top) – only visible if your role allows viewing other countries.

3. **Roadmap Overview** – four **pillar cards**. Each card shows the **Pillar Score** for the year (or **No Data**), a progress bar and the number of indicators. **Click a card** to select that pillar.

4. **Pillar panel** (left, below the cards) – the list of indicators of the selected pillar, with a search box. Each indicator shows its **Progress to Target** and a **View** link.

5. **Quick Stats** (right) – for the selected pillar and year:

   - **Total Indicators**

   - **Indicators with Data**

   - number of indicators in each status: **Maintain**, **Accelerate**, **Reverse**

   - **Pillar Score**

6. **Recent Activity** (right) – the latest answers and stories entered for your country, with who did it and when.

### 7.2 Open an indicator's detail page

1. Select a pillar card.

2. In the pillar panel, find the indicator (use the **Search indicators…** box if needed).

3. Click **View**.

The **indicator detail page** shows:

- a **Response Year** selector (change it to look at another year);

- the **Indicator Score**, the **Performance Status** with its **Achievement Ratio**, and **Assessment Details** (country, year, disaggregations completed);

- two tabs:

  - **Overview** – summary of the score, ratio and status;

  - **Disaggregation** – one row per disaggregation. **Click a row** to expand it and see the **Raw Value** you entered, the **Baseline**, the **Common Target**, the **Common Response Score**, the **Methodology**, the **Data Source**, who responded and when, and any **Notes**.
> **Tip** – This is the best place to check a single indicator after you have entered data, because it shows every number used in the calculation.

## 8. Pillars Dashboard

**Menu:** **Pillars Dashboard**.

This dashboard has three parts, from top to bottom.

### 8.1 Programmatic table (deliverables and activities)

For each pillar, a table lists your **deliverables** (first column, **Name of Deliverable**) and, on the same row, each **project / activity** (**Name of Activity/Program**). Each activity cell is coloured by its project score, and the badge next to the pillar name shows the **deliverable score** of the pillar (see section 24 for the scale).

### 8.2 Overall Country Score

The **Overall Country Score** is the geometric mean of the four pillar scores. It shows **Not Available** until **all four** pillars have a score for the year.

### 8.3 Indicators section

For each pillar, a table shows one column per year (from 2018 to the current year):

- the **pillar score** for each year, shown in the pillar header row. **Click the pillar score** to open the **score breakdown** drawer;

- one row per indicator with its **Direction** (whether an increase or a decrease is desirable) and its **score per year**. **Click an indicator score** to open the breakdown for that indicator only.

### 8.4 The score breakdown drawer

The drawer slides in from the side and shows exactly how a score was built:

- **Pillar Score** = **arithmetic mean of indicator scores**;

- for each indicator: **Indicator Score** and **Achievement Ratio**;

- for each disaggregation: **Raw Value**, **Baseline** (with year), **Target**, **Response Score**, **Data Source**, **Notes**, **Methodology** and **Attachments** (click to open the uploaded file).

Close the drawer with the **×** button.

## 9. National Dashboard

**Menu:** **National Dashboard**.

The **National Performance Dashboard** has the same layout as the Pillars Dashboard, but every number is measured against the **national targets** you configured (see section 16) instead of the common targets. The labels read **National Pillar Score**, **National Indicator Score**, **National Achievement Ratio**, **National Target** and **National Response Score**.

- Disaggregations without a national target show **No national target** and are not counted in national scores.

- The **National Overall Country Score** works like the overall score, using national pillar scores.
> **Note** – If you have not configured any national target, this dashboard will be mostly empty. That is normal; national targets are optional.

## 10. Country Comparison

**Menu:** **Country Comparison**.

1. **Select Countries** – your own country is preselected. Add other countries to compare with.

2. **Comparison Years** – pick one or several years. By default the previous year and the current year are selected. Pillar scores are averaged across the selected years.

3. **Focus Pillar** (optional) – show only one or some pillars in the table. This does **not** change the overall score, which always uses all pillars.

4. Read the summary cards: **Countries Selected**, **Avg Performance** and **Top Performer**.

5. Read the **Detailed Country Comparison** table: one row per country with each pillar score, the **Overall Score** (geometric mean of all pillar scores) and a grade.

| Grade | Overall score | Label | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | 75 – 100 | Advanced | On track to fully harness the demographic dividend |
| **B** | 60 – 74.9 | Progressing | Approaching the dividend window; momentum is building |
| **C** | 50 – 59.9 | Emerging | Just crossed the 50 % mark |
| **D** | 35 – 49.9 | Lagging | Below the 50 % mark |
| **E** | below 35 | Critical | Fundamental gaps across multiple pillars |

Countries are ranked by overall score. A country without a score for every pillar has no overall score and no rank.

## 11. Regional Comparison

**Menu:** **Regional Comparison**.

1. **Select Regions** – one or more regions (regions are groups of countries defined by the administrator).

2. **Select Countries** (optional) – to add individual countries to the comparison.

3. **Comparison Years** and **Focus Pillar** work as in Country Comparison.

4. Read the summary cards (**Regions Selected**, **Analysis Year**, **Avg Performance**, **Top Performer**).

5. Read the **Detailed Region Comparison** table: **Region**, **Overall Score**, pillar scores, **Completion** and **Status** (**MAINTAIN** for 80 and above, **ACCELERATE** for 30 to 79.9, **REVERSE** below 30). A **Country Comparison** table for the selected countries appears below it.
