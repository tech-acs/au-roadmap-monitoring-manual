# Part 5 – Success stories

Success stories are short articles about achievements and good practices. **Published** stories are visible to everyone on the public website, including visitors who are not logged in.

## 25. View stories for a pillar

1. Go to **Apps → Deliverables**.
2. Click **Manage Stories** on a pillar.
3. The page lists your country's stories for that pillar. Each card shows the title, summary, a **Published** or **Draft** badge, and **Read More**.
4. Use **Search success stories…**, the sort drop-down (**Newest First**, **Oldest First**, **Most Viewed**, **Title A–Z**) and **Clear** to find a story.

> **Note** – If your role includes the **Success Stories** menu item, that page lists stories across all pillars.

## 26. Create, edit and publish a story

**Create**

1. On the pillar's story page click **Add Story** (or **Create First Story**).
2. Under **Basic Information**:
   - **Featured Image** – click the upload box and choose a **PNG or JPG up to 2 MB**. This image is displayed on the story card and page.
   - **Story Title** (required) – a clear, compelling title.
   - **Summary** (required, max 500 characters) – a short overview shown on the card.
3. Under **Story Content**:
   - **Full Story** (required) – the complete story. Use the editor toolbar for headings, bold text and lists.
4. Tick **Publish Story** if the story is ready to be public. Leave it unticked to save a **draft**.
5. Click **Create**.

**Edit**

1. Open the pillar's story page.
2. Click the **edit** link on your story (only the author of a story can edit it).
3. Change the fields; tick or untick **Publish Story**; click **Update**.

> **Tip** – Write the story in the language selected in the interface. Keep the summary short and factual; put details, figures and quotes in the full story.

> **Important** – A published story is public immediately. Make sure it contains no confidential information and that images are cleared for publication.
