# Part 6 – Reports

The report pages appear in the menu only if your role has report permissions. Ask your administrator if you need them.

## 27. Saved report templates

**Menu:** **Apps → Reports**.

- The table lists **your** templates and templates that other users **published for everyone**, with their **Name** and **Created** date.
- Click **Download CSV** to download the report with current data.
- Click **Delete** (if allowed) to remove your own template.
- Click **Open Report Builder** to create a new one.

## 28. Build and export a report

**Menu:** **Apps → Report Builder**.

1. **Pillars (maximum 4)** – tick the pillars to include.
2. **Report Part** – choose:
   - **Indicators** – indicator data and scores, or
   - **Programmatic** – deliverables, projects, periods, budgets and results.
3. If you chose **Indicators**:
   - **Report Type** – **Indicator Summary** (one line per indicator), **Full Indicator Detail** (one line per disaggregation with raw value, baseline, target and score) or **Pillar Summary** (one line per pillar).
   - **Scoring Scope** – **Common / Global**, **National**, or **Common + National**.
4. **Geographical Range** – for country users this is **Single Country** and the **Country** field is locked to your country. (Regional averages and multi-country comparisons are available to users without a country restriction.)
5. **Years** – select one or more years (from 2018 to the current year).
6. Optional – tick **Save template**, give it a **Template Name** (for example **Togo – Pillars report 2024**) and, if you want colleagues to reuse it, tick **Publish for Everyone**.
7. Click **Preview Report** to see the columns and the first 100 rows on screen. The summary line shows the country, scope, years, pillars and total row count.
8. Click **Export CSV** to download the full report. Open it in Excel or any spreadsheet tool.
9. Click **Clear** to reset all filters.

> **Tip** – Choose **Full Indicator Detail** with **Common + National** to get every number behind the dashboards in one file.
