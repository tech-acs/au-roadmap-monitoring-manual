# AU Roadmap Monitoring Platform

## Country User Manual

**Version 1.0 · September 2026**

**Audience:** Country focal points, country administrators and data entry officers of AU member states.

---

## How to use this manual

This manual walks you through the platform **step by step**, in the order you will actually use it. You do not need to read it from cover to cover.

| If you want to… | Go to |
|---|---|
| Get started in 10 minutes | Part 1 (sections 1 – 6) |
| Understand the dashboards | Part 2 |
| **Enter your country's indicator data for a year** | **Part 3** (the main workflow) |
| Record deliverables, projects and activities | Part 4 |
| Publish a success story | Part 5 |
| Export data as a report | Part 6 |
| Create accounts for colleagues | Part 7 |
| Solve a problem | Part 9 (troubleshooting and FAQ) |
| Look up a term | Glossary (end of document) |

**Conventions used in this manual**

- **Bold text** = a button, menu item or field name exactly as it appears on screen.

- Steps are numbered. Do them in order.

- Callouts:
> **Tip** – a shortcut or good practice.
> **Note** – extra information.
> **Important** – something that can cause errors or lost work if ignored.
